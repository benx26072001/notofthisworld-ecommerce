## What each shot type means

- **front** — garment front, flat or invisible mannequin, plain seamless background
- **back** — same setup, same distance, back of the garment
- **detail** — close crop: fabric weave, print edge, stitching, hardware, label
- **editorial** — on-body, styled, moodier lighting; this is the one that sells the brand

## Specs

- Product shots (front/back/detail): **4:5 portrait, 1600×2000px**
- Editorial: **4:5 or 3:2, 2000px long edge**
- JPG quality 80. Not PNG — your current PNGs are 1.2–1.8MB each; the same shot as JPG is ~200KB.
- Same background, lighting, and camera distance across all 8 products. Consistency reads as a
  real brand more than any single beautiful shot does.
